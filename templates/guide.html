Start with evidence, not guesses

A reliable repair begins with a reproducible report. Scan the public page, record its final URL and HTTP status, measure response time, and review the page signals that visitors and search engines actually receive. Prioritize availability and security problems first, followed by mobile usability, performance, search visibility and accessibility.

Verify every correction

After making a change, repeat the same test and compare the result. Check the rendered page on mobile and desktop, confirm that important URLs return the expected status, and monitor analytics and search reporting over time. A page-level automated scan is useful for triage, while complex applications may also require authenticated, multi-page and server-side testing.
